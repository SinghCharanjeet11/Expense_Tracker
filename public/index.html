Share of all spending, all time

Every number is a real aggregation.

Nothing is added up in the browser. Totals, category shares and trends come straight from MongoDB pipelines over your own entries.

  • CRUDCreate, read, update and delete with Mongoose, with undo on delete.
  • $group and $sumTotals per category, per day, per month and per weekday.
  • Date queries$gte / $lt ranges, $dateToString and $dayOfWeek.
  • Sorting and filteringBy date, amount or title; by line, range and text search.
  • Validation and indexesRequired fields, minimums, an enum of lines, indexes on date.
